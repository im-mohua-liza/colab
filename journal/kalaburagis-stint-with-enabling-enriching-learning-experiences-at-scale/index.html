Kalaburagi's Stint With Enabling Enriching Learning Experiences at Scale

1 December 2025

At an annual district-level celebration for school leaders in Karnataka called Pragatiya Hejje, a headmistress shared a story that's stayed with the team: a student named Shaila, who faces speech challenges, stood up and confidently presented teaching materials she'd made herself — bringing her own mother to tears in the process. It's a small moment, but it's exactly the kind of change a programme like this is trying to make room for.

The problem on the ground

In North Karnataka's Kalaburagi and Yadgir districts, poor performance on 10th-grade board exams pointed to something deeper than a curriculum gap. Working through needs assessments alongside the District Institute of Education and Training (DIET), the team traced part of the problem to frequent family migration for work — which left many students feeling less connected to their school and their peers, and attendance suffered as a result.

What the response looked like

The intervention focused on two things: building school connectedness through student clubs and a student cabinet, and having students design and showcase their own teaching-learning materials (TLM). Across the Kalaburagi division, students created and presented their own material — and engagement with school visibly picked up as a result.

What "enriching" actually means here

This work fed into a broader research effort to properly define what makes a learning experience "enriching" — looking at how school, classroom and community environments together shape outcomes, and specifically at the physical, social, emotional and instructional conditions that make learning genuinely engaging for every child, not just the ones already doing well.

Teams working alongside Mantra4Change on this initiative used that research framework to identify exactly which learning experiences their programmes were actually enabling on the ground — evidence shaping design, rather than design happening first and evidence arriving after the fact.
