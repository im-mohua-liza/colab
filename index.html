Who We Are

A research, design and innovation lab founded in 2019, working at the intersection of evidence, design and collaboration to support CSOs, NGOs and funders across the Global South.

What We Do

We research real challenges, design evidence-informed solutions, and collaborate with educators, institutions and researchers to turn what works into shared, scalable public goods.

Why Choose CoLab

Building Trust Through Innovation, Evidence and Deep Ecosystem Engagement

At CoLab, we build trust through innovation and evidence-based solutions. By engaging deeply with local ecosystems, we create sustainable, impactful initiatives.
