About CoLab

Building a More Equitable Education Ecosystem

CoLab works at the intersection of research, evidence, design and collaboration to help improve education. We're a global community working to develop and disseminate evidence-informed solution packages across the Global South — as an education lab under Mantra4Change.

Why

Every child deserves equal educational outcomes.

What

Evidence-informed solutions built as digital public goods.

How

Through collaborative emergence with the ecosystem.
