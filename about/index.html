About CoLab

Building a More Equitable Education Ecosystem

Who We Are

CoLab works at the intersection of research, evidence, design and collaboration to help improve education. We're a global community working to develop and disseminate evidence-informed solution packages across the Global South — as an education lab under Mantra4Change.

Why

Every child deserves equal educational outcomes.

What

Evidence-informed solutions built as digital public goods.

How

Through collaborative emergence with the ecosystem.

"Nurture the education ecosystem to rapidly evolve ideas and approaches."

The Challenge

Why This Work Matters

Education systems across the Global South face real, persistent barriers — this is what we're working to change.

Limited Access to Local Evidence

Research is often unavailable or inaccessible, leaving practitioners without the contextual insights they need.

Disconnected Ecosystems

Knowledge remains fragmented, and connecting with the right experts or solutions is difficult.

Barriers to Scaling

Promising interventions struggle to expand across diverse contexts and communities.

Lack of Future-Ready Solutions

Innovation and foresight in programme design are often missing, limiting long-term success.
