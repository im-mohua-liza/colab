Impact Strategy

Where Evidence Meets Creativity and Usability

Our design work sits at the intersection of evidence, creativity, and usability — turning research findings and field insights into products that drive learning, adoption, and scale. We translate insights into usable tools and resources that strengthen implementation and learning.

Our design practice focuses on building a shared understanding of the what, why, and how of a program, ensuring everyone, from leadership to field teams operates with clarity and alignment.
